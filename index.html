The Problem We Solved

The Howard University Alumni Retreat faced three critical challenges that existing solutions couldn't address.

📧 Missed Sessions

Attendees relied on email schedules, leading to confusion about session times and room locations. People were showing up to the wrong rooms or missing sessions entirely—no real-time updates meant information was stale the moment it was sent.

🤝 Limited Networking

Connecting with other attendees was hit-or-miss. If you didn't catch someone during a break and exchange contact info, the opportunity was lost. There was no systematic way to discover and connect with fellow attendees before, during, or after the event.

💸 Existing Solutions Fell Short

We explored alternatives but none fit our needs:

  • Third-party conference apps required expensive subscriptions ($500-$2,000+) for a single event
  • WhatsApp groups worked for announcements but couldn't manage schedules or facilitate discovery
  • Email blasts were one-way, static, and easily overlooked

"We needed a simple, cost-effective solution purpose-built for our community."
